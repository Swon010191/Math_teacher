AI TEACHING ASSISTANT - BAN TONG HOP DU AN
===========================================

1. DINH VI DU AN
----------------

Nen tang bang giang day AI ma nguon mo, giup giao vien:

Viet hoac nhap kien thuc
-> chon noi dung
-> nhan dang
-> kiem tra bieu thuc
-> phan tich chinh xac
-> tao truc quan hoa
-> tao hoat dong tuong tac
-> AI de xuat cach giai thich va cau hoi.

San pham khong phai chatbot dat canh bang. Diem khac biet la bien noi dung
tren bang thanh trai nghiem giang day truc quan, tuong tac va do giao vien
kiem soat.


2. GIAI THICH CAC PHUONG AN A, B, C, D
--------------------------------------

2.0. Dinh nghia truc tiep bon thanh phan

A, B, C, D khong phai bon phuong an loai tru nhau. Chung la bon thanh phan
khac nhau cua cung mot san pham.


B - AI WHITEBOARD LA GI?

AI Whiteboard la ung dung bang dien tu ma giao vien mo len va su dung trong
luc giang bai. Day la man hinh lon chua toan bo bai giang.

Nhung gi giao vien nhin thay trong AI Whiteboard:

- Mot vung bang rong de viet va ve.
- Thanh cong cu gom but, tay xoa, text, chon vung va AI.
- Cac net viet tay, cong thuc, hinh anh va ghi chu.
- Cac khoi noi dung tuong tac duoc chen vao bang.
- Nut luu, mo lai va trinh chieu bai giang.

Nhung gi giao vien lam trong AI Whiteboard:

1. Viet mot cong thuc hoac noi dung len bang.
2. Chon phan noi dung muon phan tich.
3. Bam mot hanh dong nhu "Truc quan hoa" hoac "Tao cau hoi".
4. Xem ket qua xuat hien ngay tren cung mot bang.
5. Di chuyen, phong to, thu nho hoac xoa ket qua.
6. Tiep tuc viet va giang ma khong phai mo ung dung khac.

Vi du:

Giao vien viet y = x^2 - 4x + 3, khoanh cong thuc va bam "Truc quan hoa".
AI Whiteboard gui net viet di nhan dang, sau do chen mot khoi do thi tuong tac
ngay ben canh cong thuc.

AI Whiteboard la "cai bang". No quan ly khong gian, net but va moi doi tuong
tren bang. No khong tu minh tinh toan nghiem hay viet loi giai; cac engine
khac thuc hien nhung viec do.


A - INTERACTIVE TEACHING CANVAS LA GI?

Interactive Teaching Canvas la mot khoi noi dung giang day tuong tac duoc chen
vao AI Whiteboard sau khi he thong da hieu noi dung giao vien chon.

No khong phai mot bang dien tu thu hai. No giong mot "the bai giang thong
minh" hoac mot mini-app nam tren bang.

Nhung gi giao vien va hoc sinh nhin thay trong Teaching Canvas:

- Do thi, hinh hoc hoac mo phong.
- Cong thuc da duoc dinh dang ro rang.
- Cac ket qua quan trong.
- Slider de thay doi tham so.
- Nut an/hien dap an.
- Nut chuyen qua tung buoc giang day.
- Cau hoi hoac loi giai thich gan voi noi dung dang hoc.

Vi du voi y = x^2 - 4x + 3, Teaching Canvas la mot khoi gom:

+------------------------------------------------+
| Ham so: y = x^2 - 4x + 3                       |
|                                                |
|                 Do thi parabol                 |
|                                                |
| [Hien dinh] [Hien nghiem] [Hien truc]         |
| a: -----o-----  b: -----o-----  c: -----o----- |
| [Cau hoi] [Giai thich] [Buoc tiep theo]        |
+------------------------------------------------+

Khi giao vien keo slider a, do thi thay doi ngay. Khi giao vien bam "Hien
nghiem", cac nghiem x = 1 va x = 3 moi xuat hien. Nho vay giao vien co the hoi
hoc sinh truoc, sau do moi tiet lo dap an.

Teaching Canvas la noi bien kien thuc tinh thanh trai nghiem co the thao tac.
AI Whiteboard la noi chua Teaching Canvas.


D - TEACHER COPILOT LA GI?

Teacher Copilot la tro ly Generative AI hoat dong theo noi dung giao vien dang
chon tren bang. No giup giao vien tao noi dung su pham, khong phai mot chatbot
chung chung va khong phai cong cu tinh toan toan hoc.

Nhung hanh dong Teacher Copilot cung cap:

- Giai thich: viet cach giai thich phu hop voi cap hoc.
- Tao cau hoi: de xuat cau hoi nhanh cho hoc sinh.
- Tao vi du: sinh vi du tuong tu de luyen tap.
- Challenge: tao cau hoi kho hon.
- Connect: lien ket voi kien thuc lien quan.
- Teaching Sequence: de xuat thu tu nen hien noi dung nao truoc, noi dung nao
  sau.

Vi du:

Giao vien chon activity ham bac hai va bam "Tao cau hoi". Teacher Copilot co
the de xuat:

"Neu tang he so a nhung giu nguyen b va c, parabol se hep hon hay rong hon?"

Giao vien duoc xem, sua hoac tu choi cau hoi. Chi khi giao vien bam "Dua len
bang", cau hoi moi xuat hien trong Teaching Canvas.

Teacher Copilot nhan ket qua chinh xac tu Math Engine de tao loi giai thich.
No khong duoc tu tinh nghiem, tu ket luan dap an hoac tu dong dua noi dung len
bang khi chua co su xac nhan cua giao vien.

Teacher Copilot co the chay bang local AI khong can API key. Cloud AI chi la
tuy chon neu nguoi dung muon tu cung cap key.


C - OPEN TEACHING ACTIVITY / OPEN ACTIVITY PLATFORM LA GI?

Open Teaching Activity la cach dong goi mot Teaching Canvas thanh du lieu mo
de co the luu, chia se, sua va tai su dung.

Vi du, activity "Kham pha ham bac hai" khong chi la mot anh chup. No la mot
goi du lieu chua:

- Cong thuc goc.
- Ket qua tu Math Engine.
- Loai do thi can hien thi.
- Cac slider va nut dieu khien.
- Thu tu cac buoc giang day.
- Cau hoi va loi giai thich.
- Phien ban cua dinh dang du lieu.

Mot file activity co the duoc mo tren may khac va van giu nguyen kha nang
tuong tac. Giao vien khac co the thay cau hoi, sua trinh tu giang day va luu
thanh mot phien ban moi.

Open Activity Platform la he sinh thai duoc xay quanh cac file activity do,
bao gom:

- Dinh dang JSON cong khai.
- Thu vien activity do cong dong dong gop.
- Chuc nang tai ve, chia se va fork activity.
- Plugin de them mon hoc hoac loai mo phong moi.
- SDK cho lap trinh vien phat trien renderer va tool.

Vi du trong tuong lai:

- Giao vien A tao activity ham bac hai va chia se no.
- Giao vien B tai ve, doi bo cau hoi va dung cho lop cua minh.
- Lap trinh vien C tao Physics Plugin mo phong F = ma.
- Cong dong dich activity sang ngon ngu khac.

Trong MVP, Open Activity moi chi can la JSON schema, export/import va mot so
file mau. Thu vien online, marketplace va plugin SDK se duoc phat trien sau.


PHAN BIET TRONG MOT CAU

- AI Whiteboard: noi giao vien viet, chon va sap xep toan bo bai giang.
- Interactive Teaching Canvas: khoi do thi/mo phong tuong tac nam tren bang.
- Teacher Copilot: AI tao loi giai thich, cau hoi va goi y cach giang.
- Open Teaching Activity: dinh dang mo de luu, chia se va mo rong khoi tuong
  tac do.

Vi du day du:

Giao vien viet cong thuc tren AI Whiteboard -> he thong tao Interactive
Teaching Canvas -> Teacher Copilot bo sung cau hoi va loi giai thich -> toan
bo khoi noi dung duoc luu thanh Open Teaching Activity de co the dung lai va
chia se trong tuong lai.


2.1. Phuong an A - Interactive Teaching Canvas

Ban chat:

A la mot "khung hoat dong giang day tuong tac". No nhan mot noi dung kien thuc
da duoc hieu, sau do bien noi dung do thanh mot thanh phan co the quan sat,
thao tac va trinh bay trong lop. A khong phai bang ve va cung khong phai
chatbot. No giong mot widget giang day co do thi, nut bam, slider va cac buoc
tiet lo kien thuc.

Van de A giai quyet:

Neu AI chi tra ve mot doan giai thich, hoc sinh van chi doc noi dung tinh.
Interactive Teaching Canvas bien ket qua thanh trai nghiem co the thao tac.
Giao vien co the thay doi tham so, dat cau hoi, an dap an va lan luot mo tung
phan trong qua trinh giang.

Dau vao cua A:

- Bieu thuc da duoc chuan hoa, vi du x**2 - 4*x + 3.
- Loai kien thuc, vi du quadratic_function.
- Ket qua chinh xac tu Math Engine.
- Cau hinh hien thi va trinh tu giang day.
- De xuat giai thich/cau hoi tu Teacher Copilot neu AI dang bat.

He thong ben trong A thuc hien:

- Chon renderer phu hop voi loai kien thuc.
- Ve do thi hoac thanh phan truc quan.
- Tao cac control tuong tac.
- Dong bo slider voi bieu thuc va do thi.
- Quan ly thanh phan nao dang an hoac hien.
- Chuyen qua cac buoc trong teaching sequence.
- Phat su kien khi giao vien thay doi activity.

Dau ra cua A:

- Mot activity React co the chen vao whiteboard.
- Trang thai activity co the luu thanh JSON.
- Ket qua tuong tac cap nhat ngay tren man hinh.
- Mot chuoi buoc de giao vien dieu khien tiet hoc.

Vi du cu the voi y = x^2 - 4*x + 3:

1. Canvas ve parabol nhung tam thoi an dap an.
2. Giao vien hoi hoc sinh du doan truc doi xung.
3. Giao vien bam "Hien truc" de hien x = 2.
4. Giao vien bam "Hien dinh" de hien (2, -1).
5. Giao vien bam "Hien nghiem" de hien x = 1 va x = 3.
6. Giao vien keo slider a, b, c de hoc sinh quan sat do thi thay doi.
7. Math Engine tinh lai ket qua sau moi lan thay doi tham so.

Thanh phan giao dien du kien:

+------------------------------------------------+
| y = x^2 - 4x + 3                               |
|                                                |
|                  DO THI                        |
|                                                |
| [Hien dinh] [Hien nghiem] [Hien truc]         |
| a: -----o-----  b: -----o-----  c: -----o----- |
| [Buoc truoc]                    [Buoc tiep]     |
+------------------------------------------------+

Pham vi A trong MVP:

- Chi co activity ham bac hai.
- Co graph, slider va reveal controls.
- Co the keo, resize va luu tren whiteboard.
- Chua can plugin cho nhieu mon hoc.
- Chua can marketplace hoac chia se cong dong.

Vai tro da chot:

A la engine activity cot loi, duoc trien khai som va nam ben trong B. Sau nay
A co the mo rong thanh nhieu loai activity cho Toan, Vat ly, Hoa hoc va cac
mon khac.


2.2. Phuong an B - AI Whiteboard

Ban chat:

B la ung dung bang dien tu hoan chinh ma giao vien truc tiep su dung. Day la
"vo boc san pham" chua net but, text, hinh anh va cac activity cua A. B quan
ly khong gian giang day; A chi quan ly mot hoat dong tuong tac ben trong khong
gian do.

Co the hieu ngan gon:

- B la cai bang va moi thu nam tren bang.
- A la mot khoi do thi/hoat dong tuong tac duoc chen vao bang.
- D la tro ly AI tao noi dung cho khoi do.
- C la cach chia se va mo rong cac khoi trong tuong lai.

Van de B giai quyet:

Giao vien hien nay phai viet tren bang, mo trinh duyet, tim cong cu ve do thi,
copy ket qua va quay lai bai giang. B giu toan bo luong nay trong mot man hinh,
de giao vien khong bi ngat mach giang day.

Dau vao cua B:

- Net but tu chuot, stylus hoac man hinh cam ung.
- Text va cong thuc danh may.
- Vung net viet duoc giao vien chon.
- Activity da tao boi A.
- Ket qua tu Recognition va Teacher Copilot.

He thong ben trong B thuc hien:

- Quan ly toa do, zoom, pan va cac layer cua bang.
- Tao, chon, di chuyen, resize va xoa doi tuong.
- Chuyen vung net but thanh anh cho Recognition Provider.
- Hien thanh xac nhan ket qua nhan dang.
- Chen activity cua A vao dung vi tri tren bang.
- Luu va khoi phuc toan bo trang thai bai giang.
- Dieu phoi menu "Giai thich", "Truc quan hoa", "Tao cau hoi".

Golden flow cua B:

1. Giao vien viet y = x^2 - 4x + 3 bang but.
2. Giao vien khoanh hoac chon vung cong thuc.
3. B gui vung chon sang Recognition Provider.
4. He thong hien "Da nhan dang: y = x^2 - 4x + 3".
5. Giao vien xac nhan hoac sua nhanh neu sai.
6. Math Engine phan tich cong thuc.
7. B chen activity cua A ngay ben canh cong thuc.
8. Giao vien dieu khien do thi va yeu cau D tao cau hoi.

Thanh phan giao dien du kien:

+----------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Chon] [But] [Text] [Cong thuc] [Xoa] [AI] [Luu]              |
+----------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                |
|  y = x^2 - 4x + 3       +----------------------------------+   |
|                          | Activity cua phuong an A         |   |
|                          | Do thi + slider + reveal control |   |
|                          +----------------------------------+   |
|                                                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------+

Pham vi B trong MVP:

- Mot trang whiteboard local.
- But, text, select, move, resize, delete, zoom va pan co ban.
- Chon vung net but de nhan dang.
- Chen mot loai activity ham bac hai.
- Luu local va JSON export/import.
- Chua co tai khoan, cloud, multi-user hoac realtime collaboration.

Vai tro da chot:

B la san pham chinh va tam nhin dai han. Demo dau van phai trong giong mot
whiteboard, nhung chi xay nhung cong cu can cho golden flow thay vi canh tranh
day du voi cac ung dung whiteboard lon.


2.3. Phuong an C - Open Teaching Activity Platform

Ban chat:

C la he sinh thai ma nguon mo xay quanh dinh dang Teaching Activity. No khong
phai mot man hinh cu the. No la tieu chuan du lieu, bo cong cu va kho noi dung
de nguoi khac co the tao activity moi ma khong can sua truc tiep phan core.

Van de C giai quyet:

Mot nhom nho khong the tu xay activity cho moi mon hoc, cap hoc va chuong
trinh. C cho phep giao vien va lap trinh vien dong gop noi dung, widget va
plugin, giup du an mo rong boi cong dong thay vi chi boi tac gia ban dau.

C gom ba lop khac nhau:

Lop 1 - Open Activity Format:

- JSON schema co version.
- Quy dinh input, math data, widgets va teaching steps.
- Export/import khong phu thuoc mot AI provider.
- Co co che migrate khi schema thay doi.

Lop 2 - Plugin SDK:

- Plugin khai bao capability cua no.
- Plugin dinh nghia input/output schema.
- Plugin cung cap renderer hoac tool moi.
- Core kiem tra quyen va validate du lieu plugin.

Lop 3 - Community Library:

- Kho activity mau.
- Tim kiem theo mon, lop va chu de.
- Chia se, tai ve va fork activity.
- Danh gia va bao cao noi dung sai.
- Ban dich va noi dung theo chuong trinh tung quoc gia.

Vi du dong gop cua cong dong:

- Giao vien tao activity "Anh huong cua he so a den parabol".
- Mot giao vien khac fork activity va thay bo cau hoi.
- Lap trinh vien tao Physics Plugin de mo phong F = ma.
- Nhom dich thuat bo sung noi dung tieng Anh hoac tieng Viet.

Dau ra cua C:

- File activity co the trao doi.
- Plugin package co the cai dat.
- Thu vien noi dung cong dong.
- Tai lieu SDK va quy tac dong gop.

Pham vi C trong MVP:

- Chi dinh nghia Activity Model co schemaVersion.
- Co JSON export/import.
- Co mot vai activity mau trong repository.
- Chua xay plugin runtime, marketplace hay server cong dong.

Vai tro da chot:

C la huong phat trien tuong lai. MVP khong dau tu vao he sinh thai, nhung du
lieu cua A phai co schema ro rang ngay tu dau de sau nay khong phai viet lai
toan bo he thong.


2.4. Phuong an D - Teacher Copilot

Ban chat:

D la lop Generative AI ho tro giao vien ve ngon ngu va su pham. No nhan du
lieu chinh xac tu Math Engine, nguyen lieu tu activity va ngu canh lop hoc,
sau do de xuat noi dung co cau truc. D khong phai nguon su that toan hoc va
khong duoc phep tu y dieu khien whiteboard.

Van de D giai quyet:

Math Engine biet nghiem la 1 va 3 nhung khong biet nen giai thich cho hoc sinh
lop 10 nhu the nao, nen dat cau hoi gi truoc khi hien dap an, hoac nen tao vi
du tiep theo ra sao. D chuyen ket qua ky thuat thanh goi y giang day.

Dau vao cua D:

- Bieu thuc da duoc giao vien xac nhan.
- Ket qua co cau truc tu Math Engine.
- Cap hoc, mon hoc va chu de.
- Muc tieu cua giao vien: explain, question, example, challenge hoac connect.
- Tai lieu truy xuat duoc neu sau nay co RAG.

He thong ben trong D thuc hien:

- Chon prompt theo loai hanh dong.
- Goi local model hoac cloud provider tuy cau hinh.
- Yeu cau model tra ve structured JSON.
- Validate JSON bang schema.
- Loai bo hoac danh dau ket qua khong hop le.
- Hien preview de giao vien duyet.

Dau ra cua D co the gom:

- Loi giai thich ngan gon theo cap hoc.
- Cau hoi goi mo kem muc tieu danh gia.
- Vi du tuong tu co dap an do Math Engine kiem tra.
- Teaching sequence theo tung buoc.
- Goi y kien thuc lien quan.
- Canh bao khi model khong du tin cay hoac thieu du lieu.

Vi du:

Math Engine cung cap:

{
  "vertex": [2, -1],
  "roots": [1, 3],
  "axis": "x=2"
}

Teacher Copilot de xuat:

{
  "explanation": "Do thi doi xung qua duong thang x = 2.",
  "question": "Neu tang he so a, parabol se hep hay rong hon?",
  "sequence": ["show_graph", "ask_prediction", "reveal_axis"]
}

Cach D xuat hien tren giao dien:

1. Giao vien chon cong thuc hoac activity.
2. Menu hien cac hanh dong: Giai thich, Tao cau hoi, Tao vi du, Mo rong.
3. Giao vien chon mot hanh dong.
4. D tao de xuat trong nen, khong chan Math Engine.
5. Giao vien xem preview, sua neu can va bam "Dua len bang".
6. Ket qua duoc gan vao activity thay vi chi nam trong lich su chat.

Che do van hanh:

- Local AI: khong can API key, rieng tu hon, phu thuoc phan cung nguoi dung.
- Cloud AI: tuy chon, nguoi dung tu cung cap key, co the nhanh va manh hon.
- No Generative AI: Math Core, Recognition va activity van hoat dong.

Pham vi D trong MVP:

- Mot local provider.
- Ba hanh dong: Giai thich, Tao cau hoi, De xuat teaching sequence.
- Structured output va schema validation.
- Preview bat buoc truoc khi dua noi dung len bang.
- Chua can agent tu tri, RAG lon hoac knowledge graph.

Vai tro da chot:

D la chuc nang quan trong nhung khong nam tren critical path. Activity phai
xuat hien ngay tu Math Engine; D co the tai sau. Cach nay dam bao san pham van
dung duoc khi model cham, loi hoac chua duoc cai dat.

3. CAU TRUC SAN PHAM
--------------------

- AI Whiteboard: san pham va giao dien chinh.
- Interactive Teaching Canvas: chuc nang cot loi de render activity.
- Math Engine: tinh toan chinh xac, khong phu thuoc LLM.
- Recognition Engine: nhan dang chu viet va cong thuc.
- Teacher Copilot: giai thich, tao cau hoi, vi du va trinh tu giang.
- Open Activity Platform: plugin va chia se cong dong trong tuong lai.

Phuong an B (AI Whiteboard) la tam nhin chinh. Phuong an A (Interactive
Teaching Canvas) va D (Teacher Copilot) la cac he thong con. Phuong an C
(Open Activity Platform) la huong phat trien sau khi san pham cot loi da duoc
kiem chung.


4. BA CAPABILITY DOC LAP
------------------------

4.1. Math Core

Luon hoat dong, khong can Generative AI hoac API key:

- Parse bieu thuc.
- Tinh nghiem, dinh va truc doi xung.
- Sinh du lieu do thi.
- Kiem tra ket qua toan hoc.
- Render activity.
- Luu bang tren may.

4.2. Recognition

La luong su dung chinh thay vi bat giao vien nhap thu cong:

- Nhan net but hoac anh cua vung duoc chon.
- Chuyen thanh LaTeX hoac bieu thuc chuan.
- Hien thi ket qua de giao vien xac nhan.
- Cho phep sua nhanh khi nhan dang sai.

Nhap thu cong van duoc giu lam co che sua loi va du phong, khong phai trai
nghiem chinh.

4.3. Generative AI

- Giai thich theo cap hoc.
- Tao cau hoi.
- Tao vi du tuong tu.
- De xuat teaching sequence.
- Lien ket kien thuc.
- Tom tat tai lieu khi co RAG.

LLM khong duoc dung lam nguon tinh toan toan hoc. Math Engine moi la nguon
su that cho nghiem, dao ham, dinh, mien xac dinh va cac ket qua dinh luong.

7. PHAM VI TOAN HOC
-------------------

Trien khai theo thu tu:

1. Ham bac hai: y = ax^2 + bx + c.
2. Ham bac nhat: y = ax + b.
3. Ham phan thuc: y = a/x.
4. Ham sin va cos.
5. Ham logarit.
6. Ham mu.
7. Dao ham va khao sat ham so.
8. Hinh hoc.

Moc dau chi can lam sau ham bac hai:

- Do thi.
- Dinh parabol.
- Truc doi xung.
- Nghiem.
- Giao diem voi cac truc.
- Slider a, b, c.
- An/hien tung dac trung.
- Trinh tu giang day theo tung buoc.
